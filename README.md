Here is a comprehensive breakdown of Microsoft's free certifications and learning resources in 2026.

---

## 1. Completely Free: Microsoft Learn Training & Achievements

**Microsoft Learn** is Microsoft's official free learning platform with thousands of self-paced modules, learning paths, and hands-on labs.

**What's free:**
- **All training content** — interactive modules on Azure, Microsoft 365, Power Platform, AI, Security, Data, and more
- **Hands-on labs** and sandbox environments
- **Learning path completion badges** — digital achievements added to your Microsoft Learn profile
- **Practice assessments** — free sample questions to prepare for certification exams
- **Collections** — curated content you can share with teams or communities

**Note:** These are learning achievements, not formal industry certifications. However, they are verifiable and shareable on your profile.

---

## 2. Free Formal Certifications (Exam + Credential)

Microsoft certification exams are typically paid ($99–$165), but you can earn them for free through these programs:

### A. Microsoft Virtual Training Days
Free, live online expert-led sessions. **Attendees often receive a 100% free exam voucher** after completing the full session.

**Typical covered exams include:**
- Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)
- Azure Administrator (AZ-104)
- Azure Developer (AZ-204)
- AI Fundamentals (AI-900)
- Data Fundamentals (DP-900)
- Security, Compliance & Identity (SC-900)
- Microsoft 365 Fundamentals (MS-900)
- Power Platform Fundamentals (PL-900)

### B. Cloud Skills Challenges
Time-limited events (Microsoft Build, Ignite, Spring Skills Challenge) where completing a learning challenge earns you a **free exam voucher**.

**Upcoming/Recent event:**
- **AI Skills Fest** — June 8–12, 2026. Build AI skills with chances to earn prizes and **certification vouchers**.

### C. Student Free Certifications
Eligible verified students worldwide can take select **Fundamentals-level exams for free**, including:
- Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)
- AI Fundamentals (AI-900)
- Power Platform Fundamentals (PL-900)

**How to verify:** Create a student profile on Microsoft Learn and complete academic status verification.

### D. Nonprofit Organizations
Validated nonprofit organizations can access free Microsoft learning resources and training for their members.

---

## 3. Free Certification Renewal

Once you earn a Microsoft Certification, you can **renew it annually at no cost** by completing a short online assessment. This keeps your credential active without paying again.

| Platform | What's Free | Credential |
|----------|-------------|------------|

## How to Get Started

1. **Create a free profile** at [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/)
2. **Browse credentials** at [learn.microsoft.com/credentials](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/)
3. **Check for upcoming Virtual Training Days** and register to get free exam vouchers
4. **If you're a student:** Verify your academic status in your Learn profile for free fundamentals exams
5. **Join the AI Skills Fest** (June 8–12, 2026) for a chance at free certification vouchers

**Important note:** Microsoft does not offer permanent, always-free certification exams outside of these specific programs and events. The standard exam fee applies unless you qualify for one of the free voucher pathways above.
